QomSwap is a DEX running on the QL1 blockchain, providing cryptocurrency swapping, liquidity provision, staking, and governance without centralized intervention. QL1 is a Layer-1 blockchain with the QOM token as its native token, offering the following features: High Speed: Thousands of transactions per second (TPS), surpassing Ethereum (15-30 TPS).

Low Cost: Gas costs are approximately $0.00000025-$0.0000005 (Ethereum starts at just a few dollars).
Proof-of-Stake (PoS): Energy-efficient consensus.  

Memechain: Active QOM-centric community on X, Telegram, and Keet.

First-in, First-out (FIFO): Fair transaction processing.

No Memory Pool: Transactions are recorded directly into blocks, eliminating latency.



QomSwap adopts the automated market maker (AMM) model to provide users with an efficient and economical trading experience.

## 🤝 Benefits of QL1's First-In-First-Out (FIFO) Trading and No Memory Pool

QL1's technical features, FIFO trading and no memory pool, improve the user experience on QSwap.


### 1. First-In-First-Out (FIFO) Trading:

Transactions are processed in the order they are received, and priority is not affected by gas fees.
Benefits: Fairness: Small transactions (e.g., $0.01) and large transactions (e.g., $1,000) are treated equally.
Cost Predictability: Fixed gas fees ($0.00000025–$0.0000005) make budgeting easier.　　

Prevent Front-Running: Bots cannot manipulate orders (e.g., price manipulation).


### 2. No Memory Pool:

Transactions do not wait in a memory pool; they are recorded directly in blocks by validators.
Advantages: Fast Processing: Confirmations occur within 2-3 seconds (e.g., QomSwap swaps are nearly instantaneous).
Stability During Congestion: No gas price spikes or delays even during network congestion.
Security: Attacks targeting unconfirmed transactions (e.g., MEV) are reduced.
Simple Operation: The processing load on validators is reduced.

This allows QomSwap to provide advantages and fairness, especially for small-volume and high-frequency traders.
