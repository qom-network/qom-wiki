### **🐣May 29, 2024 – QL1 is Born**

Mainnet was born.  
It wasn’t just a blockchain launch, it was the beginning of something unusual.  

A decentralized ecosystem built by two passionate developers @backend69 and @BlockPhysics42. No VC funding, no infrastructure overhead, no big investor support.

Just code, belief, and a community ready to build something better.
In just 12 months, QL1 has grown from genesis to gravity, building on builders, liquidity, protocols, and attention. Progress that an entire team couldn’t achieve in years was built block by block, primarily by two people and one unstoppable chain.  

Let’s follow the path they built, piece by piece, and celebrate what true architects can accomplish when there are no obstacles in the way.  

### **🪐 May 2024 – Genesis**

May 16 – First QOM/WQOM pair created  
Sparks flew. The QOM/WQOM liquidity pair signaled that the journey from Ethereum had begun.  
May 29th – 🚀 QL1 Mainnet Launch  
The chain is up and running. Qom.one and Scan.qom.one debut. A one-way bridge connects QOM. But this is not just a tech launch. It's an escape hatch for those trapped by high gas prices and broken promises.  
Day 1. No noise. Just features.  
[Launch Post](https://x.com/QOMARMY1/status/1795784410807046487)  

May 30-31st – HAWK launch and relaunch. The community token sold out in a flash. Early demand far exceeded expectations. To meet that, HAWK was relaunched.

June 3rd QOM Migration Accelerates

$1M in liquidity before the chain even hits double digit age. No subsidies, no inflation, just trust.

Inbound, outbound — flexibility unleashed.

DeFi aggregators take notice.  
June 28th – Precipitate AI Validator vote passes  
Community-driven governance encourages protocol-level validator support. Strong early DAO movement.

### **🧨 April 30th – QomFun launches**

The QL1 meme engine has roared to life. QomFun, a Pump.fun-style meme token generator, is officially launched, allowing anyone to create and trade meme tokens in seconds. Within days, the token was all over the charts and meme culture was on fire.  

This wasn't just a tool. It was proof that QL1 could match Ethereum's meme energy without a gas tax.

After years of build delays elsewhere, the beta is live on QL1. The CAW dApp finally goes social on-chain.
